Usage terms
  You may use these marks to refer to Cupid in editorial and press coverage.
  Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the heart tile.
  Do not recolour, rotate, stretch, outline, or add effects to the mark.
  Do not use the mark to imply a partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement.
  Do not use the mark as part of your own product, logo, or domain name.
