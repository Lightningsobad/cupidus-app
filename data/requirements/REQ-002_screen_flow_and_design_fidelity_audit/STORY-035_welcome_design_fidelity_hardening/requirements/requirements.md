# Requirements: STORY-035 Hardening - Welcome Screen Visual Fidelity Evidence

Requirement: REQ-002
Retro-Type: RED-PATCH (RWB-20260816-002)
Fixes: STORY-004_welcome_design_fidelity

## Business Rationale

REQ-002's completeness audit gave the requirement a RED-PATCH verdict
because 11 DONE stories, including STORY-004, were missing rendered
visual-fidelity evidence. STORY-004's own `visual-fidelity/` directory holds
only a text report and no rendered PNGs on either side; its comparison
method read the CSS/HTML prototype source literally against the Flutter
widget tree instead of comparing rendered output to rendered output. That
same method gap is tracked as IF-7 in `req_master.md` and is the direct
cause of `BUG-20260816-001` reaching integration undetected.

This story does not restate STORY-004's original audit — it replaces the
comparison method with a rendered-output comparison, fixes anything that
method surfaces, and leaves behind the physical evidence pair the original
story was missing. A stakeholder can observe the outcome directly: the
`welcome` screen has a real rendered evidence pair proving it matches
`cupid-app-design-system`, and any deviation that pair surfaces is fixed in
the running app, not just documented.

## Scope

In scope and out of scope are fixed by the governing rework plan in
`story_master.md` (`## In Scope`, `## Out of Scope`) and are not restated
here to avoid drift between the two documents. Summary for traceability:

- Screen: `welcome` only (dark theme + `vi` locale, per STORY-004's own
  enabled configuration).
- Deliverable: a physically present, matched `implemented-welcome-<device>.png`
  / `design-reference-welcome-<platform>.png` pair in this story's own
  `visual-fidelity/` directory, plus a `visual-fidelity-report.md` recording
  `VISUAL_FIDELITY: PASS`.
- STORY-033's existing `design-reference-welcome-android.png` /
  `implemented-welcome-emulator-5554.png` pair may be reused as a starting
  baseline only if `welcome` has not changed since STORY-033 — it does not
  by itself satisfy this story's evidence-pair requirement, since the
  physical pair must exist in this story's own directory.
- Navigation/entry-point correctness for `welcome`, and any screen other
  than `welcome`, stay out of scope (owned elsewhere or tracked as separate
  rework items).
- Light theme / `en` locale visual QA (OQ5) stays out of scope — confirmed
  below, consistent with STORY-004's own precedent.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given the `welcome` screen's implementation, when its comparison against
   `cupid-app-design-system` is performed for this story, then the
   comparison method is rendered-output-to-rendered-output — a rendered
   `implemented-welcome-<device>.png` captured from the running app compared
   against a rendered `design-reference-welcome-<platform>.png` generated by
   `render-design-canvas.js --screen=welcome` — and not the CSS/HTML
   prototype source read literally against the Flutter widget tree.
2. Given the rendered comparison in AC1, when the story is delivered, then
   this story's own `visual-fidelity/` directory physically contains the
   matched, same-screen `implemented-welcome-<device>.png` /
   `design-reference-welcome-<platform>.png` pair plus a
   `visual-fidelity-report.md` recording `VISUAL_FIDELITY: PASS`.
3. Given the welcome screen rendered in the enabled dark theme + `vi`
   locale, when its rendered output is compared against the rendered
   `design-reference-welcome-<platform>.png`, then layout, spacing,
   color/token usage, and typography match exactly with no visible
   deviation.
4. Given any deviation is found by the rendered comparison in AC1-3 (as
   opposed to STORY-004's prior literal prototype-vs-widget-tree method),
   when the story is delivered, then the deviation is corrected directly in
   the app code — not merely re-documented — and re-verified by re-rendering
   both sides and re-comparing.
5. Given the sign-in and create-account CTAs on the welcome screen, when
   their rendered output is compared against the rendered design reference,
   then their styling (Dream Gradient, pill radius, platform-adaptive button
   treatment per `cupid-app-design-system` §3) matches exactly with no
   visible deviation.
