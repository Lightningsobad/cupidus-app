# Story Brief: STORY-005 Pairing

Business intent: the couple-only 1:1 pairing is the core product identity —
every other epic assumes an established pair.

Primary persona: two users forming a couple (both sides of the invite flow).

Backend contract: `invitations` module, frozen (A1).

Success signal: two accounts can pair through the app and every paired-only
feature in later epics becomes reachable only after this succeeds.
